About Me

I’m a third-year Computer Science student at Cal Poly, San Luis Obispo, focusing on Cybersecurity. I’m interested in cybersecurity, privacy, wireless/networked systems, and building software people can actually trust and depend on. What excites me most about computer science is the chance to solve challenging problems in ways that can make a real difference in people’s lives.

Through research and projects, I’ve been able to explore that interest in hands-on ways, from improving software tooling in a research lab to building applications like Homebound and helping develop a haptic feedback system for a visually impaired marching band. I’m especially motivated by work that sits at the intersection of technical depth, real-world reliability, and human impact.

Outside the classroom, photography is one of my favorite creative outlets. It helps me slow down, notice details, and bring a different perspective to both my personal and technical work.
